    Your API connection

    Use the shared Groq connection, or bring your own key. Your key stays in this tab’s memory and passes through this demo’s backend over HTTPS for each request. It is never saved in the database or browser storage.

    OpenRouter requires a model/provider supporting structured outputs and may charge your account. This demo never switches providers automatically.

    A clearer next step.

    Register your interest in this assessment demo. This does not send a request to PlacidWay or book an appointment. For a real quote, contact PlacidWay through the clinic page.

    Please do not submit medical records, symptoms or other sensitive health information.

    Privacy & data use

    This is an independent technical assessment, not an official PlacidWay service.

    Chat: your question and a short conversation history are sent to the selected LLM provider. History stays in tab memory; a new chat clears it. Do not share medical records, personal identifiers or other sensitive information.

    Logs: this demo stores outcome, source identifiers, model, token usage and latency for 30 days. For unanswered or unverified questions, it also stores up to 400 characters after basic email, phone, key and explicit name redaction. Redaction is imperfect; avoid sensitive content. Complete conversations and API keys are not stored.

    Quote form: only name, email, selected interest and consent version are stored, separately from chat logs, for up to 30 days. Requests are not forwarded to PlacidWay.

    Abuse protection: a daily salted hash of your IP is used for short-lived request counters. The application does not save raw IP addresses. Hosting and LLM providers process requests under their own policies.

    For deletion requests, contact the operator through the project repository. Do not post your email or health information in public issues.
